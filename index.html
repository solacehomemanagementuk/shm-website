FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

For Landlords

What letting support can Solace Home Management provide?

We can discuss practical support with property marketing, tenant enquiries, viewing coordination, applications and letting administration. The services available and their scope are confirmed for each enquiry.

How can you help me find a tenant?

Our tenant-finding support may include advertising a rental opportunity, handling initial enquiries and coordinating communication with prospective tenants. We can explain the proposed steps when you contact us.

Can you assist with tenant applications and referencing?

We can help coordinate application information and referencing processes. The arrangements, checks and responsibilities will be discussed with you before any support is agreed.

How do I discuss listing my property?

Visit List Your Property to learn about our upcoming listing feature. To discuss current letting or management support, contact us with the property details and the support you are looking for.

For Tenants

How can I enquire about a property?

The Browse Properties experience is being prepared, and listings are not currently available on this website. For a current property enquiry, contact us with the area and type of property you are looking for.

What information should I include in a property enquiry?

Please include the property reference, if available, and a short description of your question or requirements. This helps us understand what information you are seeking.

Who should I contact about a maintenance issue?

Use the Contact Us form to describe the issue and provide the property details. We can discuss reporting and coordination support where this is within the agreed service.

Property Management

What does your property management support include?

Support may include property-related communication, coordination and agreed day-to-day management responsibilities. The scope, responsibilities and availability are discussed before proceeding.

Can you help coordinate repairs and maintenance?

We can discuss assistance with reporting maintenance issues, communicating with relevant parties and coordinating repair arrangements. The specific arrangements depend on the service agreed.

Can you advise on property compliance requirements?

We can provide administrative assistance with identifying relevant requirements and organising related information. This is not a substitute for legal or technical advice from a suitably qualified professional.

Are your services available for every property?

Availability depends on the property, the requested support and the requirements involved. Contact us with the relevant details so we can discuss whether the service is suitable.

General Questions

How do I find out more about your services?

Visit Our Services for an overview of our property support. Contact us to discuss the scope and availability for your requirements.

How are service fees and responsibilities confirmed?

Applicable fees, service scope and responsibilities are discussed before you proceed. Any agreed terms should be clearly confirmed before the service begins.

Can I enquire about buying or selling a property?

Yes. We can discuss initial property sale or purchase enquiries and explain what support may be available. Any specialist requirements will need to be considered separately.

How can I contact Solace Home Management?

Use our Contact Us form, email solacehomemanagement.uk@gmail.com, or call 0203-432-4680.

How do I submit an enquiry through the website?

Complete the Contact Us form with your details. The online form is not yet connected to a submission service, so your enquiry will not be sent through the website. Please email us directly to submit it.
